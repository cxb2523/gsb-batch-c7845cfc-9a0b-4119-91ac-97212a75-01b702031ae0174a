Sample prompts for each modality — swap these into main.py or the individual modules.

--- IMAGE PROMPTS ---
"1) Describe this image in one sentence. 2) List the main objects you can see, comma-separated."
"What brand or product is shown in this image? What is the dominant color scheme?"
"Is there any text visible in this image? If yes, transcribe it exactly."
"Describe the mood or aesthetic of this image. Would it work for a marketing campaign?"

--- PDF DOCUMENT PROMPTS ---
"Summarize this document in 3 bullet points."
"What is the title and publication date of this document?"
"List all tables or figures mentioned in this document."
"Extract any numerical data, statistics, or benchmark results from this document."
"What are the key conclusions or recommendations in this document?"

--- VIDEO PROMPTS ---
"Describe what happens in this video in 2-3 sentences. Mention the setting and the main subject."
"What product or brand is featured in this video?"
"At what timestamp does the main action begin? Describe it briefly."
"Identify any text, captions, or on-screen graphics visible in the video."

--- AUDIO PROMPTS ---
"Transcribe the first 30 seconds of this audio clip. Then give a one-line summary of the topic."
"How many distinct speakers are in this audio? What is each speaker discussing?"
"What language is spoken in this audio? Transcribe one key quote."
"Summarize the main topic covered in this audio in two sentences."
